MYTOURBAZAR V211 - TOUR SUPPLIER EXTRACTION AND PRINT FIX

- Prevents selectable Tour PDFs from being appended and extracted twice.
- Prevents blank guest labels from consuming a PDF filename/page marker.
- Recovers travel start/end dates and transport from supplier quotations.
- Recovers multi-row hotel tables including destination, hotel, room category,
  nights, meal plan and room count.
- Calculates each hotel date range from the supplied start date and nights.
- Splits day headings into day number, date, destination/title and description.
- Stops the final day before the next page/section and removes duplicate days.
- Recovers side-by-side supplier inclusion and exclusion tables.
- Recovers flight routes, dates, timings and via points from Tour quotations.
- Recovers package totals such as "INR 71,000 per couple" with option notes.
- Keeps complete local hotel rows when the document model returns a partial set.
- Uses "Dear Guest" instead of printing an empty greeting when no guest is supplied.
