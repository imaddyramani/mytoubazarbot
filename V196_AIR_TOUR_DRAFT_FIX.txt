MyTourBazar Bot V196 - Air Print and Tour Draft Fix

AIR PRINT
- Booking-level Airline PNR is no longer repeated in every passenger row.
- The passenger table shows Ticket Number only when real passenger ticket numbers exist.
- Obvious non-name tokens such as "For" and heading fragments cannot print as travellers.
- Conflicting duplicate sectors with the same route/date/times are collapsed.
- Known airline identities are preferred when a false duplicate conflicts with a verified row.

TOUR DRAFT / GENERATION
- Every explicit supplier inclusion and exclusion is now mandatory source data.
- Supplier lists are preserved before professional rewriting or safe supplementation.
- A deterministic heading-list recovery pass restores explicit items if AI leaves either list empty.
- Recovered inclusions/exclusions flow into both the draft and generated WhatsApp/PDF output.
