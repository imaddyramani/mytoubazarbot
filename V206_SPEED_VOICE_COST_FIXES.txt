MyTourBazar V206 — Speed, Voice and Add Cost Reliability

- Free Qwen through xKiro is the primary supplier-document extraction and structuring model.
- Complete selectable supplier text is retained; PDF/image pages are sent in low-memory visual batches.
- Text-rich pages are not resent as duplicate images; Qwen reasoning is disabled for faster structured extraction.
- Tour, Air, Bus and Hotel use local rules only for source validation, calculations and outage fallback.
- Multilingual Whisper is cached during the Docker build so voice notes do not download a model at runtime.
- Voice instructions work for AI Assistant, current Tour drafts, saved-document edits and Add Cost.
- Hotel costing accepts booking-wide nightly or per-room/night rates and derives nights from stay dates.
- Air, Bus and Hotel Add Cost share one persistent, tested input and printing path.
- The private assistant can classify requests, plan workflows and answer bot questions.
- Smart edits use schema-limited patch operations and preserve unrelated booking fields.
- Northflank startup logs show the active provider and model without exposing keys.
- Telegram progress remains responsive while Qwen processes multiple page batches.
- AI timeouts, invalid JSON and provider outages fall back safely to local behavior.
- No API keys or Telegram tokens are stored in GitHub.

Deployment variables are documented in XKIRO_SETUP.md.
